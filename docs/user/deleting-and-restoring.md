# Deleting and restoring

Deleting a space, an organization, or your whole account doesn't destroy
anything straight away. You get **30 days** to change your mind, and we email
you a link that puts it all back.

## What happens when you delete something

Whichever you delete, the same three things happen:

1. **It becomes inaccessible immediately.** A deleted space and everything in it
   disappears for everyone who had access; a deleted account can't sign in.
2. **Nothing is destroyed yet.** Every board, task, page, comment and file is
   still there, untouched.
3. **We email a restore link**, valid for the full 30 days.

After 30 days it's permanently deleted, and that can't be undone.

| You delete | Who gets the restore link |
|---|---|
| A space | Every admin of that space |
| An organization | Every owner of that organization |
| Your account | You |

If you're on a paid plan, the subscription is cancelled straight away — you're
never charged for a period you asked to end. Restoring doesn't automatically
start it again; you'd re-subscribe if you want the plan back.

## Restoring

**From the email.** Open the link and press Restore. You don't need to be signed
in — which matters for a deleted account, because you can't sign in while the
deletion is pending.

**From inside Madori** (spaces and organizations). Deleted items still appear
under **Recently deleted** on the Spaces and Organizations pages, and opening
one shows a banner with a Restore button and how long you have left.

The link works once. Once someone uses it, the item is back and the link stops
working — so if you delete something a second time, use the newer email.

### If the link doesn't work

- **"Already restored"** — someone else with access already used it. Nothing
  more to do.
- **"This link has expired"** — the 30 days have passed and it's permanently
  deleted. This can't be reversed.
- **"Not valid"** — the link was probably truncated by your email client. Copy
  the whole thing from the email and paste it into your browser.

## Deleting your account

Found under **Settings → Danger zone**. You'll need to type your email and
confirm your identity, the same as any other sensitive change.

While the deletion is pending you can't sign in — use the restore link if you
change your mind. After 30 days your account is removed. Content you contributed
to shared spaces (tasks, pages, comments) stays there for the other members, but
is no longer attributed to you.

**Want a copy first?** Request an export from the same page *before* you delete —
see [Exporting your data](./data-export.md). Do it first: once the account is
pending deletion you can't sign in to request one.

### Deactivating instead

If you just want to step away, **deactivate** rather than delete. Deactivating
signs you out everywhere and hides your account, and signing back in reverses
it — no 30-day clock, nothing removed.

## What a site administrator can do

An administrator of your Madori instance can delete an account, organization, or
space — normally with the same 30-day window and restore link you'd get
yourself.

They can also delete something **permanently and immediately**, skipping the
window. That exists for two situations: honouring a legal erasure request that
can't wait, and removing abusive content. There's no undo, and every such action
is recorded with the administrator's name and their stated reason.

You'll normally be emailed when an administrator deletes something of yours,
including the reason given. If you think it was a mistake, reply to that email.
