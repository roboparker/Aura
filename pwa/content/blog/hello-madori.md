Welcome to the Madori blog. This is where we'll share **tips, tricks, and
progress updates** as we build an API-first workspace for tasks, docs, and
boards.

## What you'll find here

- **Progress updates** — what shipped, what's next, and the occasional
  behind-the-scenes on how a feature came together.
- **Tips & tricks** — getting more out of spaces, custom fields, the board
  view, and the rest of the toolkit.
- **Notes from the build** — the architecture decisions we think are worth
  writing down.

## How this blog works

Every post is a plain markdown file in the repository, so the content is
versioned right alongside the code — full history, diffs, and review on every
change. Each file carries a little frontmatter for SEO:

```markdown
---
title: A catchy headline
slug: a-catchy-headline
description: One or two sentences for search results and social cards.
date: 2026-06-21
author: Your Name
draft: false
# ogImage: /blog/a-catchy-headline.png
---

Your post body, in markdown.
```
